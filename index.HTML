📜 The Tale of the Burger Kingdom

Long ago, the Grand Feast Arena stood as a symbol of peace between every corner of the culinary world — Spicy peppers, Frozen desserts, Savory roasts, Sweet pastries, Fresh greens, and Baked breads all lived in harmony, feeding one kingdom together.

Then the Greasy Goblin seized the arena's throne, and one by one, twisted food-beasts rose up to guard it in his name. The old recipes were forgotten. The kitchens fell silent.

You are the last heir to the Burger Throne. Fight your way through every guardian standing between you and the crown that was always yours.

⚙️ Settings
Skip Animations By Rarity

When enabled for a rarity, both battle moves and Recipe Summon pulls for that rarity skip their windup/dash/impact effects and cooking or cutscene animations — damage, healing, hero reveals, and log messages still all apply instantly. Handy for speeding through content with heroes or pulls you've already seen plenty of.

Kitchen Map — Skip Spawns By Rarity

When enabled for a rarity, a wild hero of that rarity never actually appears when you'd otherwise encounter one in the Kitchen Map — you just keep walking, as if it never rolled. Other rarities still trigger normally, so you can e.g. skip past common spawns while still being stopped for anything rare or better.

❓ How to Play
  1. 🏰 Kingdom — Build kitchen buildings to earn Coins automatically over time. Each building gets pricier the more you own, so spread out your spending.
  2. 🍔 Burger Heroes — Recruit heroes from summons, then spend Crumbs to level them up. Higher rarity and level means more ATK and HP.
  3. ⚔️ Boss Battle — Tap Select Team to pick up to 15 heroes. In battle, choose a move each turn — some deal damage, others heal, shield, or debuff the boss. Stronger moves need a few turns to recharge after use (shown as ⏳ on the button), so mix in your basic attack while you wait. Match your move's food type against the boss's for bonus damage. Defeated heroes get swapped out automatically if you have a healthy one left.
  4. 📜 Quests — Complete objectives for one-time rewards. Check back as your kingdom grows — new quests unlock naturally.
  5. 📖 Recipe Summon — Spend Golden Sesame to summon new heroes. Rarer heroes are stronger; the pity counter guarantees you won't go too long without an Epic or Legendary.
  6. 👑 Royal Charter — Once you've earned enough, prestige to reset your coins and buildings for permanent Crowns, which boost everything forever.
  7. 💾 Saving — Your progress autosaves every few seconds, or tap the Save button anytime.
